Gift Buyer FAQ

What are good handmade jewelry gift ideas?

Beaded bracelets in a favorite color and one-of-a-kind pendant necklaces suit birthdays, anniversaries, weddings, bridesmaids, teachers and holidays.

Is shipping available nationwide?

Yes. Flat $4.99 shipping across the United States, and free porch delivery in the Lake Mary and Seminole County area.

Are necklaces one of a kind?

Yes. Each pendant necklace is an original and is not repeated once sold. Bracelets can be remade in the same style.

Is the jewelry sterling silver?

Many pieces use genuine sterling silver findings or chain. Materials are listed on each product page.

Can a custom or personalized piece be ordered?

Yes. Email lauren@riseandshinecreatives.com with colors, length and occasion.

How should sterling silver jewelry be cared for?

Wear often, since natural skin oils help prevent tarnish. Store in an airtight pouch, wipe with a soft polishing cloth, and avoid harsh chemicals, pools and hot springs.
